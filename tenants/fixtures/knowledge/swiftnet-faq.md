# SwiftNet — Support FAQ

## Router lights
- **Solid green** — line is synced and working.
- **Flashing green** — attempting to sync; normal for up to 3 minutes after a restart.
- **Solid red** — no line signal. Usually a line fault, not the router.
- **No lights** — check power before anything else.

## Speeds
Fibre packages sync between 60 and 90 Mbps. Anything under 30 Mbps on a fibre
package is considered a fault worth raising.

## Restart procedure
Unplug at the wall for 30 seconds, then reconnect. Allow 3 minutes to resync.
Restarting more than twice in a row does not help and can delay line retraining.

## Known limitations
- We do not support customer-owned routers.
- Wi-Fi extenders are not supplied or configured by SwiftNet.
